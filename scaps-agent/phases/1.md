PHASE 1 — metal/semiconductor contacts, 4 cases. Build (or adapt) a simple single-layer device: n-type semiconductor (e.g. Si-like: Eg 1.12 eV, chi 4.05 eV, Nd 1e16 cm-3, ~2 um) between a front and back contact; then the p-type analogue (Na 1e16). For each doping, set the front contact work function to (a) form a Schottky barrier, (b) form an ohmic contact (back contact ohmic/flat-band). Cases: n+Schottky, n+ohmic, p+Schottky, p+ohmic. Dark I-V (e.g. -1..+1 V) at 300 K for each; energy band diagram at 0 V. Save .def and results per case into runs/phase-1/.
Physics check: phi_B = phi_m - chi (n), phi_B = Eg + chi - phi_m (p); rectification only for Schottky cases; extract J0 and ideality n from forward I-V, compare J0 with A* T^2 exp(-phi_B/kT) (A* ~ 120 A cm-2 K-2 times m*), n ~ 1.
